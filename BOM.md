# Smart Inclusive Keyboard - Bill of materials

Electronic components:

1. 1 pc. [Freenove FNK0104A
board](https://docs.freenove.com/projects/fnk0104/en/latest/fnk0104/codes/MAIN/Freenove_ESP32S3_Display.html)
(2.8" LCD, no touch, with speaker)

2. 1 pc. [Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-1.47](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-1.47)

3. 1 pc. [ESP32-C3 Super Mini](https://www.espboards.dev/esp32/esp32-c3-super-mini/)

4. 1 pc. [Vibration Motor
Module](https://easyelecmodule.com/vibration-motor-module/)

Assembly:

1. Top cover: 10pc [M2.5 x 12mm Hexagonal Male/Female Standoffs](https://www.vital-parts.co.uk/threaded-hex-standoffs-male-female/11245-hmf-m25-12-s5-npb), 10pc M3.5 x 5mmPhillips Ultra Thin Flat Head Screws, 10pc M2.5 nuts.

2. Freenove FNK0104A: 4pc M3 x 6mm Hexagonal Female/Female Standoffs, 4pc M3 x5mm screws, 4pc M3 x 6mm screws.

3. The speaker has an adhesive surface and sticks to the bottom plate.

4. Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-1.47: 4pc M2 x 8mm Male/Female Standoffs, 4pc M2 x 6mm screws.

5. Vibration Motor: 2pc M3 x 10mm screws, 2pc M3 nuts.

Other components:

1. 8pc low-profile MX keyboard switchhes

2. 8pc low-profile keycaps

3. 5pc 12x6mm self-adhesive silicone bumpers
